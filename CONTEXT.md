# Cadence

A course platform: instructors publish courses made of modules and lessons; students enroll, work through lessons, and complete courses.

## Language

**Course completion**:
The moment a student's enrollment is marked as finished, recorded once with a timestamp when they complete the last remaining lesson of the course. It is never revoked, even if lessons are added later, and a course with no lessons cannot be completed.
_Avoid_: Finished, done, 100% progress

**Started**:
An enrolled student has Started a course once they have any lesson-progress record for a lesson in that course, whether or not they completed it. A student who has enrolled but never opened a lesson has not Started.
_Avoid_: Active, engaged, in progress
